# ⛽ FUELR

> **Every trade fuels your token.**

Fuelr is a token launch infrastructure designed to give creator fees
a purpose.

Instead of letting trading fees silently accumulate in a single wallet,
Fuelr lets creators define how their earnings should be distributed —
before the token starts trading.

## ⚡ What is Fuelr?

Fuelr introduces programmable hooks to token launches.

Creators can define what happens to their creator fees and automatically
route those earnings toward actions such as:

- 🔥 Buybacks & Burns
- 💎 Holder Rewards
- 💧 Liquidity
- 🏦 Treasury
- ⚙️ Custom Distribution Logic

The goal is simple:

**Every trade should have a purpose.**

---

## 🪝 Hooks

Hooks define what happens to creator earnings after trading begins.

Fuelr provides pre-built templates while also allowing creators to
define custom distribution logic.

### Standard Hooks

| Hook | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| 🔥 Buy & Burn | Use earnings to buy tokens and burn them |
| 💎 Holder Rewards | Distribute earnings to token holders |
| 💧 Liquidity | Route earnings toward liquidity |
| 🏦 Treasury | Send earnings to a designated treasury |
| ⚙️ Custom | Create your own distribution logic |

---

## 🚀 How It Works

### 1. Select a Hook

Choose a predefined distribution strategy or define your own logic.

### 2. Launch Your Token

Configure your token's name, ticker, and assets,
then deploy through the supported launch flow.

### 3. Trade Begins

Once trading starts, creator fees begin accumulating.

### 4. Execute the Distribution

The selected hook determines how those earnings are routed.

Your token's creator fees are no longer just sitting in a wallet —
they become part of the token's ecosystem.

---

## 🔍 Transparency First

Traditional token launches can leave creator fees sitting in a wallet
without a clearly defined purpose.

Fuelr changes that model.

Distribution rules can be established before trading begins,
allowing participants to understand where creator earnings are intended
to go.

**Define the destination before the journey begins.**
